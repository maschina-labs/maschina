# Support

Maschina is pre-release and run by one person. There is no support channel and no
response-time promise.

**Something is broken:** open a [bug](https://github.com/maschina-labs/maschina/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
Include the commit you are on and how to reproduce it.

**Something in the design looks wrong:** open a
[design problem](https://github.com/maschina-labs/maschina/issues/new?template=design.yml).
Most of Maschina is unproven and finding a claim that does not hold is a success,
not a complaint.

**A security issue:** do not open an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**A question about how something works:** the architecture is not public yet. The
[README](README.md) covers the shape of it, and the code is the rest.
