# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about the work, not
about the person doing it.

## What is expected

- Take the technical argument seriously and the ego lightly.
- Say what you mean directly. Bluntness is fine; contempt is not.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer, and that scope discipline is not hostility.
- Give people room to be wrong in public. That is how anything gets fixed.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.
Reports stay private. The maintainer will look into it and act, which may mean a
warning, deleting comments, or a ban.

Maschina is a small project run by one person, so this is a statement of intent
rather than a process with committees behind it.
