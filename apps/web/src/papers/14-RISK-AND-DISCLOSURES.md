## 1. Legal position

Nothing in these documents is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security, token
or financial instrument. Nothing in them is investment, financial, legal, accounting or tax advice.

Maschina does not provide investment advice, does not manage funds on behalf of users, and does not
take custody of user assets. A machine acts on instructions its owner sets. The owner is responsible for
those instructions and for the outcome.

Statements about future capability describe intent and may change. Forward looking statements
are not guarantees. Availability of specific capabilities depends on jurisdiction and may change without
notice.

Third party figures cited in these documents are attributed to their authors and are their work, not
ours. We have not independently verified them and we do not present them as our own research.

## 2. No promise of returns

Maschina does not promise, project or imply returns. It never will.

Most automated trading loses money over time. In the largest study of the question, ninety seven percent
of people who persisted at day trading lost money net of fees [3]. A machine can spend its entire budget
and return nothing.

The limits in this system protect against a machine exceeding what its owner permitted, or acting outside
its wallet policy. They do not protect against a strategy being wrong, and nothing can.

Any person or material claiming otherwise, including anyone claiming to represent Maschina, should
not be believed. We will never contact you promising a return.

## 3. Status, and what is not claimed

These documents do not state what is operational. That is published separately, dated, in the public
change log, so that it can change without these documents changing.

**Independent audit.** No independent security audit had been completed at the time of writing. When one
occurs, the report is published in full, including whatever it finds.

**Not claimed.** That any machine has been profitable. Maschina will make that claim only when it can be
made with the record attached.

## 4. Risks

**Strategy risk.** Rules that worked yesterday can fail today. This is the largest risk in the system
and it cannot be mitigated by design.

**Market risk.** Prices move violently. Slippage, thin liquidity and failed transactions cost real
money, and small trades are affected disproportionately by fixed costs.

**Protocol risk.** Machines interact with third party protocols. A failure, exploit or economic attack
in one of those protocols can cost a machine its funds regardless of how correctly Maschina behaves.

**Asset risk.** Any asset can be a fraud. Over sixty two percent of meme coins launched in 2025 were
flagged as potential rug pulls within thirty days [1]. Mandatory checks are designed to remove most of the category and
will not remove all of it. An asset can pass every check and become worthless minutes later.

**Provider risk.** Key management is performed by a third party. An outage prevents machines from
acting. A compromise would be serious, which is why the wallet policy restricts what a machine may do even if
signing were misused.

**Software risk.** Maschina is software and contains defects. Some will be found expensively.
The design bounds the consequence of a defect to a machine's own budget. That is the honest guarantee:
not that nothing will go wrong, but that what goes wrong is bounded and visible.

**Regulatory risk.** Parts of the intended system sit close to regulated activity in most jurisdictions.
Those parts arrive with a structure or do not arrive, and rules may change in ways that remove
capabilities in particular markets.

**Company risk.** Maschina is an early company and may fail commercially. Two things limit what
that would cost a user: funds are never held by Maschina, and the design goal is that they can be
recovered directly from the key management provider, without our involvement.

## 5. Tax

Trading has tax consequences in most jurisdictions and they belong to the owner. Maschina provides
exportable records of every action. It does not provide tax advice.

## 6. Commitments

We publish no returns that did not occur. We present no projection as an expectation. Every result is
shown beside the benchmark of having done nothing. What is broken is published in the change log. Audit reports are published in full including findings. Machines that failed remain visible
beside machines that worked.
