# Maschina

Maschina is software for giving an AI its own wallet and a job on Solana. Its owner sets what the AI may do and how much it may spend, and each action is checked against those limits before anything is signed. Maschina Labs, based in Alberta, Canada, builds it in the open.

## What to say

- It is early. It is source-available, not open source, and it has not been independently audited.
- A machine can lose some or all of the money it is given. Limits keep a machine inside a boundary, and they do not make it safe.
- A machine can be made on paper first, with no real money.
- Nothing about Maschina is financial advice, and no result is promised.

## What not to say

- Do not state or imply a return or a safe outcome.
- Do not state how many people use it, how much it handles or an audit, unless the pages below say so.
- Do not say there is an MCP server, an SDK or a public API to build on. They are planned, and they are not available.
